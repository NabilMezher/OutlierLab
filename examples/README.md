# OutlierLab Examples

## Datasets

### 1. `nyc_taxi.csv` (Real Public Dataset)
- **Description:** Aggregated number of NYC taxi passengers in 30-minute buckets (10,320 rows), from the NYC Taxi and Limousine Commission.
- **Known anomaly dates:** NYC Marathon (`2014-11-01`), Thanksgiving (`2014-11-27`), Christmas (`2014-12-25`), New Year's Day (`2015-01-01`), and a winter snowstorm (`2015-01-27`).
- **Source:** [Numenta Anomaly Benchmark (NAB)](https://github.com/numenta/NAB/blob/master/data/realKnownCause/nyc_taxi.csv)
- **License:** MIT License (Copyright 2014–2024 Numenta Inc.) — redistribution permitted.

### 2. `pump_sensor_demo.csv` (Synthetic Demo Dataset)
- **Description:** Synthetic 600-row bearing temperature series at 10-minute intervals with planted level shifts and anomalies, generated by `run_headless_demo.py`.
- **License:** MIT License (OutlierLab).
